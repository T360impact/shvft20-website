Governance

Governance and accountability

We are committed to acting with integrity, transparency and fairness. We declare and manage any conflict of interest, keep accurate records, and are open with the organisations, funders and communities we work with about our role and our fees.

Data protection

We handle personal information in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, collecting only what is needed, keeping it secure and using it only for the purpose it was given. Young people's data, including anything shared through our tools, is treated with particular care.

Our bid readiness check

When you ask for your full report, we keep your organisation's details, your answers and score, and your name, role, email address and phone number if you give one. We use them to send you your report and to follow up on it, which is in our legitimate interest and yours. We only send you news and offers by email, or call you about how we can help, if you tick the boxes to say we can, and you can change your mind at any time by replying to any email from us or writing to impact@shvft20.org. Your details are stored securely in the UK and EU with our database provider, Supabase, and emails are sent through Brevo. We keep them for two years after our last contact with you, then delete them. You can ask to see, correct or delete them at any time, and you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Equality, diversity and inclusion

We believe every young person deserves the same chance to thrive. We work to remove barriers, reflect the communities we serve, and make sure under-represented voices, including young people with special educational needs and disabilities, are heard and included.
