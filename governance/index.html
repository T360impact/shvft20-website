Governance

Governance and accountability

We act with integrity, transparency and fairness.

  • We declare and manage any conflict of interest.
  • We keep accurate records.
  • We are open with organisations, funders and communities about our role and our fees.

Data protection

We follow UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

  • We collect only what we need.
  • We keep it secure.
  • We use it only for the reason you gave it to us.
  • We take extra care with young people's information, including anything shared through our tools.

Our bid readiness check

What we keep
Your organisation's details, your answers and score, and your name, role and email. Your phone number too, if you give it.
Why
To send your report and follow up on it. This is in our legitimate interest and yours.
News and calls
Only if you tick the boxes to say we can. You can change your mind at any time: reply to any email from us, or write to impact@shvft20.org.
Where
Stored securely in the UK and EU with our database provider, Supabase. Emails are sent through Brevo.
How long
Two years after our last contact with you. Then we delete them.
Your rights
Ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Equality, diversity and inclusion

Every young person deserves the same chance to thrive.

  • We work to remove barriers.
  • We reflect the communities we serve.
  • We make sure under-represented voices are heard, including young people with special educational needs and disabilities.
