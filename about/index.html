About us

Communities deserve services that last.

Shvft2.0 started with a simple belief: good work should not stop when a grant ends.

Why we started

  • Funding was missed

    Strong programmes lost out, because nobody had time to build the partnership.

  • Good work stalled

    Projects stopped, because nobody planned for the second year.

  • Structure was lacking

    Committed organisations struggled without the structure to last.

So we brought together experience from local government, national charities and frontline youth work, in one independent partner.

Our mission

To strengthen the organisations that serve children, young people and communities, so they win investment, deliver with confidence and last.

Our vision

Every community served by organisations that are well led, well connected and built to last.

Our values

Young people first

We test every decision against the difference it makes to young people.

Collaboration over competition

Every organisation, large or small, has a fair voice and a clear role.

Integrity

We are honest about what is possible, and we evidence what we claim.

Built to last

We plan beyond the grant, so the work carries on.
