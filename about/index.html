About us

Communities deserve services that last.

Shvft2.0 was founded on a simple principle: effective community services should outlast the funding cycle.

Why we started

  • Missed investment

    Strong programmes lose out on funding when no one has the capacity to build the partnership.

  • Stalled delivery

    Valuable work stalls when no one plans for the second year.

  • Fragile foundations

    Committed organisations struggle without the structures to sustain themselves.

We brought together experience from local government, national charities and frontline youth work into a single, independent partner.

Our mission

To strengthen the organisations and partnerships that serve children, young people and communities, so they secure investment, deliver with confidence and sustain their impact.

Our vision

Every community served by organisations that are well led, well connected and built to last.

Our values

Young people first

Every decision is tested against its impact on the young people our partners serve.

Collaboration over competition

Every organisation, regardless of size, has a fair voice and a clear role.

Integrity

We are candid about what is achievable and evidence every claim we make.

Built to last

We plan beyond the grant so that impact endures.
