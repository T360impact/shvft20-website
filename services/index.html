Services

Ten services, one joined-up journey.

Use one service, or several together. Tap "What is included" to see more.

01

Funding and bids

We find the right funding and help you make a strong, honest case for it.

What is included
  • Funding research and tracking
  • Testing which opportunities are right for you
  • Bid management and bid writing
  • Budgets and financial design
  • Working with funders, including due diligence
02

Consortium and partnerships

We bring organisations together into fair, well-run partnerships that funders trust.

What is included
  • Finding and assessing partners
  • Compliance and safeguarding checks
  • Consortium agreements
  • Agreed ways of working
  • Leading the consortium for the life of a grant
03

Commercial partnerships

We help you build lasting relationships with businesses.

What is included
  • Partnership strategy
  • Introductions to businesses
  • Sponsorship and corporate giving
  • Joint programmes
  • Social value partnerships
  • Relationship management
04

Programme design

We help shape programmes that meet people's needs and funders' priorities.

What is included
  • Programme review and redesign
  • Delivery models
  • Who the programme is for, and who is eligible
  • Theory of change
  • Risk assessment and risk mapping
05

Impact reporting

We help you measure and show the difference you make, as one partnership.

What is included
  • Outcomes and impact frameworks
  • Rolling them out across teams and partners
  • Data collection systems
  • Reporting to funders, boards and communities
06

Operations

We design how a service runs, then help make it happen safely.

What is included
  • Operational design and site readiness
  • Recruiting your first staff
  • Coordinating the workforce
  • Safeguarding and health and safety on the ground
  • Registration and referrals
  • Finance tracking
  • Youth voice
  • Out-of-hours emergency cover
07

Marketing and brand

We help you find a clear voice and reach the people you serve.

What is included
  • Brand identity
  • Brand language and messaging
  • Programme launches
  • Outreach campaigns
  • Communications for partners and stakeholders
08

Capacity and sustainability

We help you build foundations, so you do not depend on the next grant.

What is included
  • Organisational structure
  • Long-term strategy
  • Contingency planning
  • Team design
  • Fundraising strategy and training
  • Building a business development function
09

Workforce and leadership

We develop your people, from youth workers to senior leaders.

What is included
  • Management and leadership training
  • Leadership coaching
  • Team culture
  • Youth work training
  • Safeguarding practice
  • Progression into senior roles
10

AI skills and adoption

We help you use AI safely, to give your team time back.

What is included
  • AI readiness assessments
  • AI policy and governance
  • Safe and ethical use training
  • AI for bids, reporting, communications and admin
  • Hands-on projects in everyday work
  • AI literacy sessions for young people
Who we work with

Everyone who shapes a community's future.

  • Lead organisations

    Foundations and charities who want a partner to build and run their partnership.

  • Councils and commissioners
