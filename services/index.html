Services

Ten services, one joined-up journey.

Each service can be commissioned individually or as part of an integrated programme.

01

Funding and bids

Identifying the right opportunities and building a compelling, credible case for investment.

Scope
  • Funding research and tracking
  • Testing which opportunities are right for you
  • Bid management and bid writing
  • Budgets and financial design
  • Working with funders, including due diligence
02

Consortium and partnerships

Convening fair, well-governed partnerships that funders trust.

Scope
  • Finding and assessing partners
  • Compliance and safeguarding checks
  • Consortium agreements
  • Agreed ways of working
  • Leading the consortium for the life of a grant
03

Commercial partnerships

Building lasting, values-led relationships with business.

Scope
  • Partnership strategy
  • Introductions to businesses
  • Sponsorship and corporate giving
  • Joint programmes
  • Social value partnerships
  • Relationship management
04

Programme design

Designing programmes that meet community need and funder priorities.

Scope
  • Programme review and redesign
  • Delivery models
  • Who the programme is for, and who is eligible
  • Theory of change
  • Risk assessment and risk mapping
05

Impact reporting

Measuring and evidencing impact consistently across a partnership.

Scope
  • Outcomes and impact frameworks
  • Rolling them out across teams and partners
  • Data collection systems
  • Reporting to funders, boards and communities
06

Operations

Designing service operations and supporting safe, effective mobilisation.

Scope
  • Operational design and site readiness
  • Recruiting your first staff
  • Coordinating the workforce
  • Safeguarding and health and safety on the ground
  • Registration and referrals
  • Finance tracking
  • Youth voice
  • Out-of-hours emergency cover
07

Marketing and brand

Developing a clear identity and voice that reaches the communities you serve.

Scope
  • Brand identity
  • Brand language and messaging
  • Programme launches
  • Outreach campaigns
  • Communications for partners and stakeholders
08

Capacity and sustainability

Building the structures that reduce reliance on any single grant.

Scope
  • Organisational structure
  • Long-term strategy
  • Contingency planning
  • Team design
  • Fundraising strategy and training
  • Building a business development function
09

Workforce and leadership

Developing leaders, managers and frontline youth workers.

Scope
  • Management and leadership training
  • Leadership coaching
  • Team culture
  • Youth work training
  • Safeguarding practice
  • Progression into senior roles
10

AI skills and adoption

Adopting AI safely and responsibly to release capacity for frontline work.

Scope
  • AI readiness assessments
  • AI policy and governance
  • Safe and ethical use training
  • AI for bids, reporting, communications and admin
  • Hands-on projects in everyday work
  • AI literacy sessions for young people
Who we work with

Everyone who shapes a community's future.

  • Lead organisations

    Foundations, national charities and established providers seeking an independent partner to build and coordinate partnerships.

  • Councils and commissioners

    Public bodies seeking a single accountable partner and dependable reporting.

  • Funders

    Investors seeking well-governed grantees that can evidence their impact.

  • Grassroots groups

    Community organisations seeking to join larger partnerships and build capacity.

  • Businesses

    Companies seeking credible community partnerships that deliver measurable social value.
