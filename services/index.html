Services

Ten services, one joined-up journey.

Ten services that can stand alone or work together as one joined-up journey, shaped around what each organisation and community needs.

01

Funding and bids

We help organisations find the right opportunities and make a strong, honest case for investment. We test each opportunity before work begins, so effort goes where it can genuinely succeed, and we work with funders directly from the first question to final confirmation.

IncludesFunding research and tracking, opportunity qualification, bid management, bid writing, budgets and financial design, funder liaison and due diligence support.
02

Consortium and partnerships

The biggest opportunities now ask organisations to deliver together. We act as an independent convenor, bringing grassroots groups, established charities, local authorities and businesses into partnerships that are fair, well governed and trusted by funders.

IncludesPartner identification and assessment, compliance and safeguarding due diligence, consortium agreements, ways of working frameworks, Partnership Consortium Lead role for the life of a grant.
03

Commercial partnerships

We help organisations build lasting relationships with business, rooted in shared values and mutual benefit, so they have support they can rely on beyond any single grant.

IncludesPartnership strategy, corporate introductions, sponsorship and corporate giving, joint programmes, social value partnerships, relationship management.
04

Programme design

We work with organisations to shape and strengthen their programmes so they meet the needs of the people they serve and the priorities of those who invest in them.

IncludesProgramme review and redesign, delivery models, cohort and eligibility design, theory of change, risk assessment and risk mapping.
05

Impact reporting and frameworks

We help organisations understand and evidence the difference they make. Where several organisations deliver together, we make sure everyone measures in the same way, so the partnership tells one clear story and learns as it goes.

IncludesOutcomes and impact frameworks, implementation across teams and partners, data collection systems, reporting to funders, boards and communities.
06

Operations

We design how a service should run and then help make it happen, so doors open on time, young people are safe and every session counts.

IncludesOperational design, site readiness, recruiting first staff, workforce coordination, on-the-ground safeguarding and health and safety, registration and referrals, finance tracking, youth voice, out of hours emergency cover.
07

Marketing and brand

We help partnerships and organisations find a clear identity and voice, and reach the young people and communities they exist to serve.

IncludesBrand identity, brand language and messaging, programme launches, outreach campaigns, communications for partners and stakeholders.
08

Capacity building and sustainability

We help organisations put lasting foundations in place, so they are not dependent on the next grant to survive.

IncludesOrganisational structure, long-term strategy, contingency planning, team design, fundraising strategy and training, building a business development function.
09

Workforce, leadership and youth work development

We invest in the people behind the work, from managers and senior leaders to the youth workers closest to young people.

IncludesManagement and leadership training, leadership coaching, team culture, youth work training and upskilling, safeguarding practice, progression into senior roles.
10

AI skills and adoption

AI can give small teams back the time they need for young people and communities, but only if it is used safely, ethically and with confidence. We help organisations understand what AI can do for them, build the skills to use it well, and put the right safeguards in place, especially where young people's information is involved.

IncludesAI readiness assessments, AI policy and governance, safe and ethical use training, practical skills for bids, reporting, communications and admin, hands-on projects that build AI into everyday workflows, and AI literacy sessions for young people.
Who we work with

Everyone who shapes a community's future.

Lead organisations

Foundations, national charities and established providers with the standing to hold major grants, who want an independent partner to build and coordinate the partnership so their own teams can focus on delivery.

Local authorities and commissioners

Councils and public bodies that want a borough partnership built and run well, with one accountable point of contact and reporting they can rely on.

Funders and foundations

Investors who want the organisations they back to be well structured, well led and able to evidence their impact.

Grassroots and community organisations

Smaller groups closest to young people and communities, who want to take part in larger partnerships and build the capacity to grow.

Businesses

Companies that want to invest in their communities through meaningful partnerships that deliver real social value.
