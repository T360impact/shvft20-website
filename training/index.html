Training and courses

Developing the people who make the work happen.

Practical, practitioner-led training for the people who make youth and community work happen. Courses can be delivered in-house for your team or as open sessions, and tailored to your organisation or partnership.

Youth work development

Core youth work practice, safeguarding and engagement skills, and upskilling for youth workers moving into lead and senior roles.

Leadership and management

Programmes for new managers, senior leaders and emerging leaders, building confidence, decision-making and the ability to lead teams through change.

Mindset and performance

High performance coaching and mindset development for leaders and teams, drawing on our founder's work as an accredited executive coach.

Funding and partnership readiness

Workshops on building partnerships, making a strong case for investment, working with funders and developing commercial relationships.

Youth voice in practice

Training for staff and young people on setting up and running youth councils, advisory boards and youth-led research.

AI for the youth and community sector

From safe everyday use for all staff to practical skills for fundraising, impact reporting and communications, plus strategy and governance sessions for leaders and trustees.
