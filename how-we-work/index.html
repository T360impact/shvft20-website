How we work

One partner from the first conversation to long-term impact.

A five-stage approach, delivered by one team throughout, so nothing falls between separate consultants.

  1. 01

    Listen

    We build a clear understanding of your organisation, your community and your ambitions.

  2. 02

    Qualify

    We assess every opportunity rigorously before work begins. A clear no protects your time and reputation.

  3. 03

    Build

    We convene the right partners, strengthen programmes and review every policy, including safeguarding.

  4. 04

    Secure

    We lead the bid, design the budget and work with the funder through to confirmation.

  5. 05

    Deliver and sustain

    We remain engaged: leading the partnership, reporting on impact and building for the long term.

Our principles

What you can count on.

Partners agree first

No commitment is made to a funder without each partner's agreement.

Evidence, not inflation

Accurate figures, drawn from each organisation's own data.

Clear scope and cost

Scope and fees are agreed at the outset.

Young people's voices

Youth voice is built into every partnership, from design to delivery.

Youth voice

Shaped with young people, not for them.

Young people are experts in their own lives.

  • Youth councils and boards

    We help organisations establish youth councils and advisory boards. Our founder established a London borough's youth council, including a dedicated board for young people with special educational needs and disabilities (SEND).

  • Co-designed programmes and bids

    Young people contribute to programme design and to the bids that fund them.

  • Continuous feedback

    Through Fully Heard, partners gather young people's views throughout delivery.
