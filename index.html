Remarkable work deserves the structure to last.

Across the sector, organisations are doing remarkable work with too little time, structure or support behind them. Funding increasingly asks for partnerships, evidence and long-term plans that small teams struggle to put together while still running services.

Shvft2.0 is an independent partner that holds the whole journey with them. We bring the right partners together, help secure the investment, support delivery on the ground and build the foundations that keep good work going long after a grant ends.

From idea to impact.

Most support stops at the application. We stay through mobilisation, delivery and reporting, so what was promised is what young people receive.

An independent convenor.

We are not tied to any one organisation, which lets us bring councils, charities, grassroots groups and businesses together around a shared purpose and hold the partnership fairly.

Practitioner-led.

Our team has managed youth hubs, worked inside local government and raised funds for national charities. We know what commissioners look for and what delivery really takes.

Built to last.

Every piece of work is designed for sustainability, with the structure, people and relationships an organisation needs beyond its next grant.

Our impact

The shift, measured.

Our team has helped bring more than £3 million of investment into youth and community work, through national charities, local government and partnerships of local organisations.

Local programmes built to last

Around £300k

Working within local government, our founder secured around £300,000 for youth and community programmes. Several are still running and sustaining themselves today, because they were designed for the long term from the start.
