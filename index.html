About us

Communities deserve services that last.

Our story

Shvft2.0 was founded on a simple observation: communities deserve services that last. Too often we have seen strong youth and community programmes miss out on investment because nobody had capacity to build the partnership, valued work stall because nobody planned for the second year, and committed organisations struggle because they lacked the structure to sustain themselves.

We set out to change that, bringing together experience from local government, national charities and frontline youth work into one independent partner that stays with organisations from the first conversation to long-term impact.

Our mission

To strengthen the organisations and partnerships that serve children, young people and communities, so they can secure the investment they need, deliver with confidence and sustain their impact for the long term.

Our vision

Every community served by organisations that are well led, well connected and built to last.

Our values

Young people first.

Every decision is tested against the difference it makes to the people our partners serve.

Collaboration over competition.

We build partnerships where every organisation, large or small, has a fair voice and a clear role.

Integrity.

We are honest about what is achievable, we never commit partners to what they have not agreed, and we evidence what we claim.

Built to last.

We plan beyond the grant, so the work continues when the funding cycle ends.

How we work

One partner from the first conversation to long-term impact.

Nothing falls between separate consultants.

  1. Step 1

    Listen.

    We start with your organisation, your community and your ambitions: the difference you want to make, who is already around you, and what is holding you back.

  2. Step 2

    Qualify.

    We test every opportunity honestly before work begins, looking at eligibility, the funder's priorities, the wider landscape and your capacity to deliver. A clear no at this stage protects your time and your reputation.

  3. Step 3

    Build.

    We bring the right partners together, strengthen their programmes, check every policy and safeguarding document, and agree how the partnership will work and measure its impact.

  4. Step 4

    Secure.

    We manage and write the bid, design the budget, and work directly with the funder until investment is confirmed.

  5. Step 5

    Deliver and sustain.

    We stay. We lead the partnership, support operations where needed, report on impact, and help build the people, brand and relationships that keep the work going beyond the grant.

Our principles

Partners agree before we commit.

Nothing is offered to a funder on a partner's behalf until that partner has agreed it.

Evidence, not inflation.

We count young people accurately, use each organisation's own data, and make claims we can stand behind.

Clear scope and cost.

We agree the scope and cost of our work at the outset, so there are no surprises along the way.

Young people's voices shape the work.

We build youth voice into every partnership, from design to delivery.

Youth voice

Shaped with young people, not for them.

Young people are not an afterthought in our work. They are experts in their own lives, and the best programmes are shaped with them, not for them.

Our founder built a London borough's youth council, with a dedicated board for young people with special educational needs and disabilities. We bring that experience to every partnership: helping organisations set up youth councils and advisory boards, involving young people in programme design and bids, and making sure their feedback keeps shaping delivery once the work begins.

Through Heard, our youth voice platform, partners can hear from young people continuously, not just at the start of a project.

Governance

Governance and accountability

We are committed to acting with integrity, transparency and fairness. We declare and manage any conflict of interest, keep accurate records, and are open with the organisations, funders and communities we work with about our role and our fees.

Data protection

We handle personal information in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, collecting only what is needed, keeping it secure and using it only for the purpose it was given. Young people's data, including anything shared through our tools, is treated with particular care.

Equality, diversity and inclusion

We believe every young person deserves the same chance to thrive. We work to remove barriers, reflect the communities we serve, and make sure under-represented voices, including young people with special educational needs and disabilities, are heard and included.
