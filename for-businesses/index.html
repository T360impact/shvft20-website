For businesses

Connect your values with real local impact.

Many businesses want to make a real difference in the communities where they work, but find it hard to know where to start, which organisations to trust, or how to connect community impact with their own values and goals. We help close that gap.

We start with your business: your values, your people, your customers and your objectives. Then we connect you with the local charities and community organisations whose work genuinely fits, and help shape partnerships that create lasting change for young people and communities while reflecting who you are as a business.

Finding the right partners

We know the local youth and community sector well, so we can introduce you to trusted organisations that match your purpose and your places.

Designing meaningful partnerships

From community investment and employee volunteering to mentoring, skills programmes and joint campaigns, we help design partnerships with clear goals on both sides.

Telling the story well

We help you communicate your community impact authentically to staff, customers and stakeholders, building on our founder's experience of corporate partnerships with a national charity.

Evidencing social value

We help you measure and report what your partnership achieves, supporting your social value commitments and responsible business reporting.
